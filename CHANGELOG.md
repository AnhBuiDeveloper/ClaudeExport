# Changelog

Customer-facing release status, checked on **22 October 2026**. Store approvals and staged rollouts can differ by browser. A website announcement or a source version does not establish store availability.

## Published: 1.1.1 confirmed in Chrome

The [Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/claude-export-to-pdf-docx/eppnfcohgdbnmnekhfcpgokapbggmfde) showed version **1.1.1**, with an updated date of **4 September 2026**. This is the listing's update date, not a claim about every user's installation date.

The published listing describes:

- Individual-response and full-conversation exports, with readable headings, lists, tables and code.
- Artifacts included with answers and optional inclusion of thinking.
- Free core exports, five full-conversation exports every 30 days and five Pro trial exports per install.
- Pro advanced formats, bulk export, custom ranges, templates, redaction, document controls and Send to Notion.
- Automatic activation after checkout and email-based purchase restore.

The website describes 1.1.1 as preserving the export behavior of 1.1.0. No earlier store release dates are reconstructed here.

## Announced / scheduled: 1.2.0 — store availability unconfirmed

The [official website changelog](https://claude.export.anhbui.dev/changelog) labels **1.2.0** a scheduled release for **21 October 2026**. This date is an announcement, not confirmation of approval or publication in either store. Chrome still showed 1.1.1 when checked; Edge's published version was not independently confirmed. Do not interpret this section as a released-version announcement.

Publicly announced workflows include:

- Searchable PDF through the browser's native print dialog.
- Visual message and artifact selection and additional document controls.
- Word image handling and missing-image recovery.
- Revised Free attribution and trial rules; see [Free vs Pro](docs/free-vs-pro.md).
- User-assisted full and incremental backups, resume, and an optional searchable local archive.
- English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Vietnamese interface options.

Use these workflows only when they appear in your installed version. Google Docs availability depends on a configured, available connection and is not promised for current store installations.

## Check your version

Open `chrome://extensions` or `edge://extensions`, find Claude Export, and view its details. Check the relevant [Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/claude-export-to-pdf-docx/eppnfcohgdbnmnekhfcpgokapbggmfde) or [Edge listing](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/claude-export-to-pdf-doc/cmkoamjlhidmbdgamojbkjllgmbbielg) for the version offered to you.
