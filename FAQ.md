# Frequently asked questions

For availability differences, read the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md). Chrome's confirmed listing was 1.1.1 on 22 October 2026; website guides can describe scheduled 1.2 workflows.

## Is Claude Export free?

Yes. Core exports need no account signup. Free includes individual-response exports, a limited number of full-conversation file exports and five Pro trial exports per install. Saved Free files in the 1.1.1 listing carry attribution; clipboard text does not. See [Free vs Pro](docs/free-vs-pro.md).

## Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. Install from the official links in the [installation guide](docs/installation.md). Other browsers are not promised here.

## Can I export individual responses?

Yes. Choose the latest response or use the available inline Export control. This is useful when you want one answer rather than the whole thread.

## Can I export full conversations?

Yes. Free includes five full-conversation file exports per rolling 30 days. Pro removes that limit and offers custom message ranges. The scheduled 1.2 rules distinguish downloads/copies from basic native PDF printing; see [the plan comparison](docs/free-vs-pro.md).

## What document formats are available?

Free includes PDF, Word/DOCX, Markdown, TXT and clipboard. Pro adds HTML, JSON and ZIP, plus document controls. Trial credits let you try eligible advanced exports. See [exporting conversations](docs/exporting-conversations.md).

## Does the extension upload my conversations?

Standard exports are generated locally. Optional Send to Notion transfers your chosen content directly to Notion. If an available Google Docs connection is explicitly enabled, chosen content goes to Google. Licensing, billing, support and product events have their own data handling; see [privacy and data](docs/privacy-and-data.md).

## How do I restore a Pro purchase?

Install the extension in the browser profile you want to use, open the popup and choose the purchase-restore option. Use your purchase email and the one-time verification code privately. You can also follow the [official activation guide](https://claude.export.anhbui.dev/activate). For unresolved problems, email [support@anhbui.dev](mailto:support@anhbui.dev); never post your code or license key.

## How do Free and Pro differ?

Pro adds unrestricted full-conversation exports, advanced formats, bulk ZIP export, custom ranges, templates, redaction, document controls and Notion transfer. The website also describes newer backup/archive features for 1.2. See [Free vs Pro](docs/free-vs-pro.md) for the version boundary and [pricing](https://claude.export.anhbui.dev/pricing) for current purchase terms.

## How do backups work?

Published Pro bulk export saves selected accessible conversations into a ZIP. The website's scheduled 1.2 workflow adds user-assisted full/incremental backup, resume and an optional local searchable archive. It is not confirmed available in both stores. See [backup and archive](docs/backup-and-archive.md); exported files and local records should be backed up separately.

## Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Use the license-management link from your purchase email to cancel an eligible monthly or yearly subscription at period end. Contact [support@anhbui.dev](mailto:support@anhbui.dev) privately if needed. The one-time plan has no subscription renewal. Check [current terms](https://claude.export.anhbui.dev/term).

## How do I report a problem?

Read [troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md), then submit a [sanitized bug report](https://github.com/AnhBuiDeveloper/ClaudeExport/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Use private email for billing, account, privacy or security matters. Do not include conversations, credentials, license tokens, purchase records or payment information publicly.
